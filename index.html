Questions

Before you ask.

Do I own the files, website and accounts?+

Yes. Your domain, logins, raw footage and final files are in your name. We hand everything over when the work is done.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on what you need, so every project gets one fixed quote after a short call. You know the full price before any work starts.

Can I hire you for just one thing?+

Yes. Many clients start with one reel or one website. You add more only if you want to.

Do you work outside Nellore?+

We shoot in Nellore and nearby towns. Editing, websites, SEO and software we do for clients anywhere.

How do changes work?+

You review on a private link and comment on the exact moment or spot you want changed. Rounds of changes are agreed in your quote.

Can we talk in Telugu?+

Yes. Telugu or English, on calls, in messages and in the content we make for you.
